The Problem

MENA brands run influencer campaigns blind, across 3 disconnected tools.

🔎

Discovery is guesswork

Global databases are weak on Arabic, dialects & local platforms (Snapchat, TikTok-Arab). Shortlisting still happens over DMs, spreadsheets & agencies with opaque pricing.

📋

Campaigns live in chaos

Outreach, approvals, contracts, content review, payments & reporting scattered across inboxes — unscalable past ~10–15 creators.

📊

Zero competitive visibility

Brands can't see which creators competitors use, what they spend, or share of voice — the most-requested, least-served insight.

⚠️

Compliance risk

In KSA, using an unlicensed (non-Mawthooq) creator carries fines up to SAR 5M. Current tools don't flag it.
