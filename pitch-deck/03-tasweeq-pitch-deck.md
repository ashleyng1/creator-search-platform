## Slide 2 — Problem

**MENA brands run influencer campaigns blind, across three disconnected tools.**

- **Discovery is guesswork.** Global databases are weak on Arabic, dialects, and the platforms
  that matter here (Snapchat, TikTok-Arab, local KOLs). Brands still shortlist via DMs,
  spreadsheets, and agency word-of-mouth with **opaque pricing.**
- **Campaigns live in chaos.** Outreach, approvals, contracts, content review, payments, and
  reporting are scattered across inboxes and sheets — unscalable past ~10–15 creators.
- **Zero competitive visibility.** Brands can't see which creators their competitors use,
  what they spend, or their share of voice — the single most requested, least-served insight.
- **Compliance risk is real.** In KSA, using an unlicensed (non-Mawthooq) creator carries
  fines up to **SAR 5M**. Current tools don't flag it.

> Result: brands overpay agencies, stitch 2–3 foreign tools that don't speak Arabic, and
> fly blind against competitors.

---

## Slide 3 — Solution

**Tasweeq unifies the three jobs into one MENA-native workflow.**

| Pillar | What it does | Replaces |
|---|---|---|
| **🔎 Search** | Natural-language Arabic/English brief → ranked, verified creators. Filters on audience geo, dialect, engagement, fake-follower score, **Mawthooq status**. | Modash + manual DMs |
| **📋 Manage** | Shortlist → team feedback → approver gate → outreach templates → contracts → payments/escrow → post-performance analytics. | Upfluence / GRIN + spreadsheets |
| **📊 Compare** | Track any competitor's creator roster, estimated spend/EMV, share of voice, and content themes over time. | HypeAuditor / Kolsquare / Lucidya |

- **The wedge:** the *first transaction* — a marketer types an Arabic brief and gets a
  campaign-ready, compliance-checked shortlist in minutes, then benchmarks it against a rival.
- Working MVP already live (search → shortlist → approval → outreach → analytics → insights).

## Slide 4 — Why Now

**The MENA creator economy just became a regulated, funded, high-growth sector — and no
software has consolidated it yet.**

- **Vision 2030:** KSA targets **SAR 47B (~$12.5B)** media output and **150,000 jobs**;
  creator economy is explicit national policy.
- **Regulation created a clean, verifiable supply:** Mawthooq licensing (10,000+ issued) cut
  the KSA commercial creator pool **~35%** — fewer, professional, *verifiable* creators. A
  compliance/verification dataset foreign tools can't legally replicate.
- **Behavior:** ~99% internet penetration, ~70% under 35, TikTok reaches ~88% of Saudis,
  world-leading Snapchat use. KSA creator economy **grew ~32% in Q1 2025 alone.**
- **Event supercycle:** Riyadh Season → Expo 2030 → **World Cup 2034.** Early entrants pay
  2–3x less for equivalent reach.
- **AI cost curve** makes Arabic dialect NLP + benchmarking economically viable now.

*Sources: Arab News, GAMR/Mawthooq, Statista, Kolsquare (cite on slide).*
