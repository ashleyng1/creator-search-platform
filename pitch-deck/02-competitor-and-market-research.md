# Tasweeq — Competitor & Market Research (Deep Dive)

> Research pack for the Tasweeq investor pitch. Tasweeq (تسويق, Arabic for "marketing") is a
> MENA-first influencer platform with three pillars: **Search** (discover creators),
> **Manage** (run campaigns end-to-end), and **Compare** (benchmark competitor influencer
> strategy). This document maps the global and MENA competitive landscape, the market size,
> and where Tasweeq wins.

**Important on sourcing:** Numbers below are pulled from public market reports, vendor sites,
funding databases, and trade press (Statista, Mordor Intelligence, Influencer Marketing Hub,
Kolsquare, PS Market Research, GII, Tracxn, TechCrunch, Arab News, GAMR/Mawthooq coverage).
Market-sizing figures from different firms use different scopes and **disagree by design** —
always cite the source and the year on the slide, and treat everything here as
"directionally correct, verify before print." Ranges are given where sources conflict.

**Regional structure:** The GCC market is officially described as **"fragmented"** with
Saudi Arabia the largest country and the UAE the fastest-growing — i.e. **no dominant
software winner has consolidated the region yet.** That is the central white-space thesis.

### 1.3 "Why now" tailwinds (MENA)

- **Vision 2030 media targets:** KSA targets **SAR 47B (~$12.5B)** in media-sector output
  (~0.8% of GDP) and **150,000 jobs**. Creator economy is an explicit policy priority.
- **Regulation is professionalizing supply:** The **Mawthooq** license (via GAMR, launched
  Oct 2022) is mandatory for anyone earning from promotional content in KSA — SAR 15,000 for
  3 years, fines up to SAR 5M. **10,000+ creator licenses issued.** It **cut the active KSA
  commercial influencer pool ~35%** (hobbyists exited) — leaving a smaller, more professional,
  more expensive, and *verifiable* pool. Compliance filtering is a **feature**, not a burden —
  and a data moat a Western tool cannot easily replicate.
- **Digital penetration:** KSA ~99% internet penetration; ~70% of population under 35;
  38.6M social identities (~111% of population); TikTok reaches ~88% of Saudis; Snapchat
  penetration among the highest globally.
- **Event supercycle:** Riyadh Season, Expo 2030 (Riyadh), and FIFA World Cup 2034 (KSA)
  lock in years of brand + creator spend. Early entrants pay 2–3x less for equivalent reach.
- **Q1 2025 KSA creator economy grew ~32% in a single quarter** (Admitad/Stllr via Pyrafluence).

## 2. The Competitive Landscape

The market splits into **four functional categories**. Tasweeq's wedge is that it fuses
three of them (Search + Manage + Compare) in one MENA-native product, where incumbents force
buyers to stitch 2–3 tools together.

### 2.1 Category A — Discovery / Search-first platforms

| Platform | DB size | Strengths | Gaps (vs. Tasweeq) | Pricing (approx.) | Funding |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Modash** | 250M+ profiles (IG/TikTok/YT) | Largest DB, deep audience filters, fake-follower checks, 50+ data points | Discovery-first; light campaign mgmt; no competitor benchmarking; not MENA/Arabic-native | From $199–$500/mo | ~$14M total ($12M Series A, 2024, henQ) |
| **HypeAuditor** | 200M+ profiles | Industry-standard fraud/authenticity scoring (~94% accuracy), competitor analysis module | Priced for enterprise; global-generic; weak Arabic/dialect; not full workflow | ~$10k/yr | — |
| **Favikon** | 9 platforms incl. LinkedIn | AI intent search, unified cross-platform profiles, scoring/ranking, competitor research | Global/B2B lean; not MENA-native; no Arabic dialect NLP | Mid-market | — |
| **Collabstr** | Marketplace | Book creators directly, no negotiation | Marketplace only; no analytics depth; no benchmarking | Transaction-based | — |

### 2.2 Category B — All-in-one campaign management suites (the "Manage" incumbents)

| Platform | Strengths | Gaps (vs. Tasweeq) | Pricing | Funding |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **CreatorIQ** | Enterprise "Creator Intelligence Cloud", API integrations, brand-safety, measurement suite. Customers: Disney, Unilever, Sephora, H&M | Enterprise-only pricing/complexity; global-generic; no MENA/Arabic depth; not for SMB/mid-market | Enterprise (custom) | ~$80M total (Series D $40M, 2021) |
| **GRIN** | Best-in-class Shopify/e-commerce creator management, 200M+ profiles | E-comm/DTC-centric; US-centric; higher entry cost | From ~$399/mo | ~$144M total |
| **Upfluence** | Mature all-in-one: discovery, outreach, affiliate, gifting, payments; customer-to-creator discovery | Priced for established teams; global-generic | From ~$478/mo | — |
| **Aspire** | Two-sided marketplace, ambassador programs, creators apply to briefs | Gifting/ambassador lean; ~$2,000/mo entry; not MENA | ~$2,000/mo | — |
| **Traackr / Influencity** | Enterprise measurement; Influencity adds reach-dedup + competitor analysis | Global-generic; enterprise workflow | Enterprise / from mid tier | — |
| **Later Influence** | Predictive AI on verified purchase data (results-driven) | Ties to Later's social suite; US-centric | Bundled | — |

### 2.3 Category C — Competitor benchmarking / social listening (the "Compare" pillar)

This is the fastest-growing and least-integrated capability — and the core of Tasweeq's
differentiation. Today it lives in *separate* tools from discovery/management.

| Tool | What it does | Limitation for a MENA brand |
|---|---|---|
| **HypeAuditor Competitor Analysis** | Full influencer list of a rival's campaign, creatives used, estimated budget, CPE/EMV, hashtags | Instagram-centric; global; separate purchase from workflow |
| **Kolsquare Listening** | Track competitor KOL activations, share of voice, positioning by market/vertical | Enterprise; Europe-first; add-on module |
| **Influencity Competitor Analysis** | Mentions, sentiment, benchmark brand performance | Global-generic |
| **influencermarketing.ai (IMAI)** | Brand vs. top-3 competitors, share of voice, sentiment, influencer impact | Global-generic |
| **Sprout Social / Rival IQ / Hootsuite / Socialinsider** | Cross-channel competitive benchmarking, alerts, sentiment | Broad social suites, not influencer-campaign-specific; weak Arabic |
| **Yoloco / Archive / Shortimize** | Track influencer ad placements / organic+paid content / short-form video | Point solutions |

**Insight:** No leading platform delivers **Search + Manage + Compare in one MENA-native
workflow.** Brands currently pay for Modash (search) **+** Upfluence/GRIN (manage) **+**
HypeAuditor/Kolsquare (compare) — three subscriptions, three logins, none Arabic-first.

### 2.4 Category D — MENA-native / regional players

| Player | What it is | Positioning vs. Tasweeq |
|---|---|---|
| **Lucidya** (KSA) | Arabic-first marketing & brand intelligence; sentiment across **17 Arabic dialects** (~92% accuracy); social listening, competitor benchmarking, influencer identification | Strong on *listening/intelligence* (overlaps "Compare"), **not** a discovery-to-campaign workflow. Potential partner or benchmark, and proof that Arabic-NLP moats are fundable. |
| **ArabyAds** (MENA) | Enterprise e-commerce/ad-tech suite; **ArabyAds Talent** for celebrity/influencer mgmt; acquired **indaHash**; **raised $30M pre-Series B** (AfricInvest) | Agency + ad-tech model; enterprise/performance media. Less a self-serve SaaS for brand marketing teams. |
| **MoonTech** (KSA/UAE) | AI influencer marketing platform for KSA & UAE; discovery + campaign mgmt + ROI; claims launch in <24h, Arabic+English support | Closest direct MENA competitor on Search+Manage; **no explicit competitor-benchmarking pillar**. |
| **Hypeo AI** (MENA) | AI matching + collaboration platform for brands & creators; "match in <5 min" | Early; matching/collaboration focus; not benchmarking-led. |
| **Grynow / Ykone / agencies** | Managed-service influencer agencies in KSA/UAE | Service, not product; opaque pricing — the status quo Tasweeq disrupts. |
| **Keepface / Pyrafluence** | Regional campaign platforms with escrow/payouts (Pyrafluence: 5% fee, same-day payout) | Transactional/marketplace; Pyrafluence supports only IG/YouTube verified creators. |

## 3. Competitive Positioning — Where Tasweeq Wins

### 3.1 The gap (one sentence)
> Global tools are powerful but **not MENA-native** (weak Arabic/dialect NLP, no Mawthooq/
> compliance layer, no local platform depth on Snapchat/TikTok-Arab, and priced/built for
> US enterprise). MENA players are either **listening-only** (Lucidya), **agency/ad-tech**
> (ArabyAds), or **early single-pillar** (MoonTech, Hypeo). **Nobody unifies Search + Manage
> + Compare in one Arabic-first product.**

### 3.2 Feature comparison matrix (for the "Competition" slide)

| Capability | Modash | CreatorIQ | HypeAuditor | Lucidya | MoonTech | **Tasweeq** |
|---|:--:|:--:|:--:|:--:|:--:|:--:|
| Creator **Search**/discovery | ✅ (best) | ✅ | ✅ | ◐ | ✅ | ✅ |
| Campaign **Manage** (shortlist→outreach→approval→payment→analytics) | ◐ | ✅ | ✗ | ✗ | ✅ | ✅ |
| Competitor **Compare** / benchmarking | ✗ | ◐ | ✅ | ✅ | ✗ | ✅ (core) |
| **All three in one workflow** | ✗ | ✗ | ✗ | ✗ | ◐ | ✅ |
| **Arabic-first / dialect NLP** | ✗ | ✗ | ✗ | ✅ | ◐ | ✅ |
| **Mawthooq / GCC compliance** layer | ✗ | ✗ | ✗ | ✗ | ◐ | ✅ |
| **Snapchat + TikTok-Arab** depth | ◐ | ◐ | ◐ | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |

✅ = strong · ◐ = partial · ✗ = weak/absent. (Verify each cell before presenting; competitor
roadmaps change.)

### 3.3 Tasweeq's moats (what a VC will ask "why can't Modash just do this?")
1. **Arabic-first data + dialect NLP** for brief-to-filter parsing and sentiment (mirrors
   Lucidya's proven-fundable moat, applied to full workflow).
2. **Compliance/verification graph** built on Mawthooq licensing + local platform data —
   a regulatory dataset foreign entrants can't legally assemble the same way (non-Saudi
   creators must operate via licensed local agencies).
3. **The "Compare" flywheel:** every campaign run on Tasweeq enriches the competitor-benchmark
   dataset (share of voice, EMV, creator overlap) — a proprietary data flywheel, the exact
   "AI defensibility" 2026 VCs look for, not a generic AI wrapper.
4. **Local GTM + payment rails:** same-region escrow/payout, Arabic/English support, alignment
   to the Saudi cultural calendar (Ramadan, Riyadh Season, National Day, Founding Day).
