# The VC Pitch Deck Playbook & Checklist (2026)

> What top VCs actually want in a deck, distilled from Sequoia/YC templates and 2026
> deck-review data (Waveup 1,000+ decks, DocSend eye-tracking, elev-x, SeedForge, DECKO,
> valueaddvc). Use this as the rubric for the Tasweeq deck in `03-tasweeq-pitch-deck.md`.

---

## 1. The hard rules (non-negotiables)

- **Length: 10–12 slides** for seed/Series A. 15 is the absolute max; >15 signals you can't
  prioritize. Everything else goes in the appendix / data room.
- **Attention budget:** VCs scan a deck in **under ~3 minutes** on first pass, ~20–40 seconds
  before deciding to keep reading. Investors now spend ~22% *less* time per deck than a year
  ago. **Every slide must land one point instantly.**
- **DocSend time-on-slide data — invest effort accordingly:**
  Financials (~23%) > Team (~19%) > Traction (~15%) get the most attention.
  Competition, market size, and GTM get the least. **Put 80% of your polish into the top 5:
  Traction, Team, Solution/Product, Business Model, Financials.**
- **Proof over narrative (the 2026 shift):** Replace research-report claims with *named
  customers, signed LOIs, month-over-month data, and a specific ask tied to milestones.*
  Investors now run AI over decks to extract and test claims — unverifiable claims get filtered.
- **One idea per slide. Big font. Charts up-and-to-the-right. Cite sources in small text.**
- **Lead with your strongest signal.** If traction, team pedigree, or a killer "why now" is
  your edge, put it early rather than defaulting to problem-first.

## 2. The 11–12 slide structure (with the "proof signal" each must carry)

| # | Slide | Its ONE job | Proof signal VCs scan for | Common 2026 mistake |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Title / Hook** | One-line vision + category | Memorable, specific positioning | Vague tagline, no category |
| 2 | **Problem** | Real, painful, named | Named customers / quantified pain | Generic "market is broken" |
| 3 | **Solution / Product** | How you kill the pain | Product shot + the first transaction | Feature list, no demo |
| 4 | **Why Now** | Timing is unfair to competitors | Regulatory/behavioral/tech shift | Missing entirely |
| 5 | **Market (SOM-first)** | The slice you can win in 18 mo | **Bottom-up** SOM, not top-down TAM bubble | $50B TAM bubble chart |
| 6 | **Traction** | Proof people want it | MoM growth, retention, LOIs, pilots | Vanity totals, no retention |
| 7 | **Business Model** | How you make money | Unit economics, **LTV:CAC ≥ 3:1**, payback | No unit economics |
| 8 | **Go-to-Market** | Repeatable channel | One channel that works + CAC | "We'll do everything" |
| 9 | **Competition** | Why you win / defensibility | Honest matrix + your moat | "We have no competitors" |
| 10 | **Team** | Why *you*, why now | Founder-market fit, domain insight, prior wins | Advisor logos as substance |
| 11 | **Financials** | Credible path | 3-yr projection tied to assumptions | Hockey stick with no basis |
| 12 | **The Ask** | Capital → milestones | "$X to reach [milestone] by [date]" | "Raise to grow the team" |
| + | **Appendix** | Depth on demand | Full model, roadmap, cohort data | (Put detail here, not in main) |

## 3. Slide-by-slide standard-to-clear (2026 bar)

- **Problem:** Name a real buyer and quantify their pain (time/money wasted, tools stitched).
- **Solution:** Show the product and the *first transaction / core loop*, not a feature grid.
- **Why Now:** A shift that makes this fundable *today* — new regulation, platform behavior,
  cost curve. (For Tasweeq: Vision 2030, Mawthooq, event supercycle, AI cost drop.)
- **Market:** Lead with **bottom-up SOM** ("N target brands × ACV = $Y reachable in 18 mo"),
  then layer SAM and TAM. Drop the top-down bubble.
- **Traction:** Chart that goes up-and-to-the-right. Pre-revenue? Show pilots, LOIs, waitlist
  conversion, design partners. Pair market with proof: "12,000 target teams → 47 convos,
  11 LOIs, 3 closed."
- **Business Model:** State pricing, ACV, gross margin, **LTV:CAC** and payback period. The
  3:1 LTV:CAC line is a basic capital-efficiency signal.
- **Competition:** Honest 2x2 or feature matrix. Show competitors' *actual* workarounds
  (spreadsheets, 3 stitched tools) and your defensible moat.
- **AI narrative (if any):** Show where AI creates **defensibility** — a proprietary data
  flywheel or cost/workflow advantage. "Undifferentiated AI wrappers are getting passed over."
- **Team:** "Why you, why now" via domain-specific insight only you could have — not pedigree.
- **Financials:** Projections tied to explicit assumptions; state when unit economics turn
  positive and on what basis.
- **Ask:** Connect the raise to milestones and runway. Fundable: *"Raising $2M to reach $1M
  ARR by Q1 2027."* Not fundable: *"Raising $2M to grow the team and expand."*

## 4. Pre-send checklist (tick before it leaves your outbox)

- [ ] 10–12 slides; appendix carries the rest
- [ ] Story survives a 3-minute silent scan (readable without you narrating)
- [ ] Strongest signal appears in the first 3 slides
- [ ] Every claim has a source citation or a data point behind it
- [ ] Market slide is **bottom-up SOM**, not a top-down TAM bubble
- [ ] Traction shows MoM growth / retention / LOIs (not just totals)
- [ ] Business model shows LTV:CAC (target ≥ 3:1) and payback period
- [ ] AI/defensibility = a real moat (data flywheel), not "we use AI"
- [ ] Competition slide is honest and shows the moat
- [ ] Team slide answers "why you, why now" with earned insight
- [ ] Ask ties $ to specific milestones + runway
- [ ] One idea per slide, big fonts, consistent brand, no typos
- [ ] Financials are the most-polished slide (they get the most VC attention)
- [ ] A separate short **teaser deck** exists for cold outreach; the full deck for meetings

---

## 5. The three deck variants (know which you're sending)
1. **Teaser deck (5–8 slides):** cold outreach; must stand alone in an inbox.
2. **Investor deck (10–12 slides):** this document's target; sent before/after first meeting.
3. **Presentation deck:** what you talk over live (lighter text, you fill the gaps).

The Tasweeq deck in `03-tasweeq-pitch-deck.md` and `index.html` is built as the **investor
deck** and doubles as the presentation deck.
